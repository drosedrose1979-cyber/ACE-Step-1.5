Straight answers

What business owners want to know.

Why did I get a link to a website for my business?

We build previews for businesses that don't have a strong presence online yet. The preview is yours to look at. If you want it, reply and we'll take it from there. If you don't, tell us and we'll take it down.

Can you guarantee I'll rank #1 on Google?

No. Google's own guidance says no one can guarantee a #1 ranking, so treat anyone who promises one as a red flag. We do the groundwork local search depends on and keep it maintained.

Where do you work?

Anywhere. Everything happens online, so your location is never a barrier.

I run a franchise location. Can you help?

Franchisors usually control their locations' websites and listings, so we check that first. If yours does, we'll tell you before any work starts.
